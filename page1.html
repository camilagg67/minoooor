Welcome to the ultimate BLACKPINK fan hub — your one-stop destination for everything about the biggest girl group in the world. Whether you are a Blink since their debut in 2016 or you just discovered them through "Shut Down," this site is made for you. BLACKPINK, formed by YG Entertainment, consists of four incredibly talented members: Jisoo, Jennie, Rosé, and Lisa. Together, they have redefined what it means to be a global K-pop sensation, breaking records, headlining festivals, and selling out stadiums across continents.

BLACKPINK debuted on August 8, 2016, with the single album Square One, featuring the explosive tracks "Whistle" and "Boombayah." From that moment, the world knew something special had arrived. Their unique blend of fierce raps, powerful vocals, and stunning visuals set them apart. Over the years, they have released hit after hit, including "Playing with Fire," "Kill This Love," "How You Like That," "Pink Venom," and "Shut Down." Each comeback brings a new concept, a new fashion statement, and a new reason to love them.

What makes BLACKPINK truly iconic? Here are just a few reasons:


  • They are the first K-pop girl group to headline Coachella, performing in 2019 and returning as headliners in 2023.

  • Their music videos have accumulated billions of views on YouTube, with "DDU-DU DDU-DU" becoming the first K-pop group video to surpass 2 billion views.

  • They have collaborated with global superstars like:
    • Lady Gaga,
    • Selena Gomez,
    • Cardi B,
    • Dua Lipa


  • Each member has a successful solo career, proving their individual artistry and star power.

  • They are ambassadors for major luxury brands, including Chanel, Dior, Saint Laurent, and Celine.

The bond between BLACKPINK and their fandom, BLINK, is unbreakable. BLINKs are known for their dedication, creativity, and global presence. From streaming parties to fan projects, from concert banners to social media trends, BLINKs support the group in every possible way. The name "BLINK" itself is a combination of "BLACK" and "PINK," symbolizing the group's dual concept — fierce and sweet, powerful and elegant.

On this website, you will find detailed profiles of each member, a full discography with descriptions of every release, and an interactive online quiz to test your knowledge as a true BLINK. So grab your lightstick, put on your favorite BLACKPINK track, and explore everything we have prepared for you. Let's dive into the world of BLACKPINK — where the music is loud, the fashion is bold, and the queens rule.

Stay tuned for more updates, and remember: BLACKPINK in your area!

BLACKPINK is not just a K-pop group — they are a global phenomenon that has shattered every barrier imaginable. Their journey from a small practice room in Seoul to the biggest stadiums in the world is a story of hard work, passion, and unwavering dedication. Every performance, every music video, and every album is a testament to their relentless pursuit of excellence.

One of the most remarkable things about BLACKPINK is their ability to constantly reinvent themselves while staying true to their signature sound. Each era brings a fresh concept — from the fierce, badass energy of "Kill This Love" to the elegant, nostalgic vibe of "Shut Down." They experiment with genres, push creative boundaries, and always surprise their fans. This versatility is exactly why they have remained at the top of the global music scene for years.

Let's take a closer look at some of their most unforgettable achievements:

  1. Coachella 2023 — BLACKPINK became the first K-pop act ever to headline the iconic festival, delivering a historic two-hour set that left the world speechless.


  2. YouTube Records — Their channel has over 90 million subscribers, making them one of the most subscribed music artists on the platform.


  3. Billboard Charts — Born Pink debuted at No. 1 on the Billboard 200, a first for a K-pop girl group.


  4. World Tours — The Born Pink World Tour spanned 34 cities across the globe, selling over 1.8 million tickets.


  5. Fashion Influence — Each member represents a different luxury house, solidifying their status as global fashion icons.


But beyond the numbers and the awards, what truly sets BLACKPINK apart is their authenticity. They are not afraid to be themselves — bold, funny, vulnerable, and powerful all at once. The bond between BLACKPINK and BLINK is not just about music — it is about mutual respect, shared dreams, and a connection that transcends distance and time.
